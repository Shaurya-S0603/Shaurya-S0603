## Featured Projects

### [QuantEdge | Quantitative Research & Backtesting](https://github.com/Shaurya-S0603/Quant-Edge-Terminal)

A quantitative equity research platform for historical strategy development and evaluation across US and Indian NSE markets.

- Implemented trend-and-volatility and moving-average strategies, next-open execution, transaction costs, portfolio accounting, and benchmark comparisons.
- Conducted an exploratory study covering 16 stocks, two ETFs, six walk-forward checks, and a 2,000-path moving-block bootstrap.
- Maintained a separate November 2024–October 2026 holdout outside interactive research. The exploratory results showed lower drawdowns and lower returns than the fixed-weight benchmark, without establishing a statistical edge.

`Python` · `JavaScript` · `Quantitative Research` · `Backtesting` · `Statistical Evaluation`

[Repository](https://github.com/Shaurya-S0603/Quant-Edge-Terminal) · [Research methodology](https://github.com/Shaurya-S0603/Quant-Edge-Terminal/blob/main/research/yahoo-development-replay.md)

### [Ashwing | Competitive Poker AI](https://github.com/Shaurya-S0603/PokerBots)

A No-Limit Texas Hold'em agent and interactive poker platform exploring strategic decisions under incomplete information.

- Developed a custom poker engine, Monte Carlo equity estimation, opponent modelling, and expected-value-based decisions.
- Combined a precomputed heads-up response policy with adaptive play and short-stack equity analysis.
- Implemented reproducible strategy benchmarks, hand-history replay, action validation, and a Docker-based Chipzen deployment.

`Python` · `Monte Carlo Simulation` · `Game Theory` · `Streamlit` · `Docker`

[Repository](https://github.com/Shaurya-S0603/PokerBots) · [Play Ashwing Poker](https://ashwingpoker.streamlit.app/) · [Ashwing v2 on Chipzen](https://chipzen.ai/bots/49375fc1-7b6d-4b7e-966f-44600ed28fb5)

### [Minecraft Manhunter AI | Autonomous Pursuit Agent](https://github.com/Shaurya-S0603/Minecraft-Manhunt-AI)

An autonomous Survival-mode hunter for Minecraft Java Edition, developed using Python, Fabric, and Carpet.

- Implements rolling 3D A* navigation, terrain-aware pursuit, obstacle recovery, and vision-limited tracking.
- Integrates resource gathering, cost-checked crafting, melee combat, swimming, climbing, bridging, and portal traversal.
- Uses contextual playbook learning and isolated training scenarios with held-out evaluation to investigate improvements in pursuit and movement.

`Python` · `A* Search` · `Adaptive Control` · `Autonomous Agents` · `Fabric / Carpet`

[Repository](https://github.com/Shaurya-S0603/Minecraft-Manhunt-AI)
