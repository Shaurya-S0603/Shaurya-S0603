## About Me

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at **Nanyang Technological University, Singapore**, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, game development, 3D design, and data-driven software. I enjoy turning ambitious ideas into interactive products - from AI-powered architecture tools and business-intelligence platforms to games and real-time 3D experiences.

I primarily build with **Python, Unity, and Blender**. My experience spans software development, AI/ML, data analytics, game design, 3D modelling, animation, and technical leadership. I also co-founded **myRide**, where I contribute to Unity-based visualisation features, 3D models, and animation.

## What I'm Building

### 🏗️ ArchAI

An AI-driven architecture platform that generates 2D floor plans and converts them into interactive 3D models. The project combines generative design, BIM-aware workflows, real-time cost estimation, material suggestions, site analysis, and VR walkthroughs.

`Unity` `Python` `PyTorch` `Blender` `React` `Firebase` `VR` `BIM`

### 📊 SellerPilot

An enterprise-grade business-intelligence platform that transforms raw marketplace sales data into actionable insights. It automates data ingestion and reporting while providing interactive dashboards for revenue, product performance, and regional sales analysis.

`Python` `Pandas` `Streamlit` `Plotly` `SQLAlchemy` `SQLite`

### 📈 Quant Edge

An interactive financial analytics platform for exploring historical market data, technical indicators, company comparisons, portfolio performance, and data-driven market summaries.

`Python` `Pandas` `Streamlit` `Plotly` `SQLite` `Financial APIs`
